The Problem

India loses ₹10,000+ crores annually to cyber scams. Deepfakes are weaponized for fraud, blackmail, misinformation, and impersonation attacks against citizens, banks, and public figures.

🎭

Deepfake Videos

AI face-swapped videos of celebrities and officials used for fraud, fake news, and non-consensual content.

🗣️

Voice Cloning Scams

"Your son is in trouble" scam calls using cloned voices — fooling parents and companies into wire transfers.

📱

WhatsApp / SMS Phishing

Fake KYC, lottery, delivery, and job offer messages steal OTPs, credentials, and bank details daily.

🌐

Fake Websites

Typosquatted bank, UPI, and government portals harvest logins and drain accounts within minutes.

Our Solution

A multi-modal AI detection platform accessible via Web, Mobile, Chrome Extension, and Telegram/WhatsApp Bot — with explainable verdicts and government-grade reporting.

🎬

Video Deepfake Detection

XceptionNet + FaceForensics++ pipeline detects face-swap, lip-sync mismatch, and temporal inconsistencies with Grad-CAM heatmap.

🖼️

Image Forensics

GAN-artifact detector spots AI-generated faces, morphed IDs, and photoshopped documents.

🎙️

Voice Anti-Spoofing

Wav2Vec2 + RawNet2 analyze spectrogram anomalies to catch cloned and synthesized voice.

💬

Text Scam Classifier

Fine-tuned BERT + LLM reasoning flags phishing patterns, urgency triggers, and impersonation across Hindi + English.

🔗

URL Threat Intel

Real-time domain reputation, typosquatting detection, SSL check, and Safe Browsing integration.

🛡️

Explainable AI

Every verdict comes with visual highlights and reasoning — building trust for citizens and courts.

🧩

Chrome Extension

Right-click any social media post → instant SAFE / FAKE badge inline.

🤖

WhatsApp / Telegram Bot

Forward suspicious content to bot → get verdict in seconds. Zero learning curve.

📊

Cybercrime Dashboard

Case management, geo-heatmap, PDF reports — built for police cybercells and banks.
